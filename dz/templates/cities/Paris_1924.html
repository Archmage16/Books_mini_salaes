Paris in 1924 was a vibrant center of culture, fashion, and art, recovering from the effects of World War I. This period, known as the “Roaring Twenties” or Les Années Folles in French, was marked by the rise of jazz, literature, and modern art. The city attracted famous writers and artists such as Ernest Hemingway, Pablo Picasso, and F. Scott Fitzgerald, who made Paris a hub of creativity. In the same year, the 1924 Summer Olympic Games were held in Paris, strengthening its image as an international city of peace, sports, and innovation.
