Paris is the capital and most visited city of France,
often called the City of Light. It is famous for its
romantic atmosphere, elegant architecture, and world-class
museums such as the Louvre and Musée d’Orsay. The Eiffel
Tower, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and Champs-Élysées are among
its most iconic landmarks. Paris is also a global center
for fashion, art, and gastronomy, making it a symbol of French culture and sophistication.
