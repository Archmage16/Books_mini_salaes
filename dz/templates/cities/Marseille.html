Marseille, located on the Mediterranean coast, is the oldest city in
France and the country’s main port. Known for its multicultural
atmosphere and lively energy, Marseille combines French and North African
influences in its food, music, and traditions. The city’s highlights include
the Old Port (Vieux-Port), Basilique Notre-Dame de la Garde, and the nearby
Calanques National Park, famous for its stunning sea cliffs. Marseille’s mix
of history, nature, and diversity gives it a unique southern charm.
